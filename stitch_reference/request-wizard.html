Institutional Security Directive

OFFICIAL WARNING TO ALL STUDENTS AND APPLICANTS

Please read these terms carefully before initiating any document request. Violations are tracked through digital audit logs and reported to the PLMun Student Disciplinary Board and City Legal Office.

1.

False Information and Fraudulent Applications

Submitting falsified identity documents, unauthorized grade alteration claims, or misrepresenting enrollment status constitutes academic fraud. Incurring violations results in immediate suspension, expulsion, cancellation of academic credentials, and criminal prosecution under Republic Act No. 10175 (Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012) and the Revised Penal Code of the Philippines.

2.

Unauthorized Document Requests & Identity Theft

Official transcripts, diplomas, and certifications are strictly confidential. Attempting to request documents on behalf of another student without an authenticated Special Power of Attorney (SPA) or notarized authorization will trigger an automatic security lock on the student profile.

3.

Protection of Personal Information (Republic Act No. 10173)

The University Registrar complies with the Data Privacy Act of 2012. Personal data provided herein will be processed exclusively for document verification, printing, archival tracking, and physical scheduling under strict encryption.
